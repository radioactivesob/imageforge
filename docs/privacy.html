IMAGEFORGE 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version: ImageForge 3D collects nothing. Your photos, spools and designs stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ImageForge 3D has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests and sends no data, including your photos, to us or to anyone else.

Photos and camera

When you choose a photo or take one with the camera, ImageForge 3D reads it on your device to design your print. Photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded. The app only sees the photos you pick.

Where your data lives

Your spool library and settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it and no way to access it.

Sharing

When you export a 3MF file, test chip or print sheet, the app creates it on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, such as Files, AirDrop or your slicer. ImageForge 3D never uploads it anywhere itself.

Children

Because the app collects and transmits nothing, no personal information about children, or anyone, is ever gathered by us.

Changes

If a future version of ImageForge 3D ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated here before that version is released.
